# Modular male-human milestone

See the [source preparation, full inventory, palette modes and provenance contract](../assets/human_male/README.md).
The runnable recipes are `configs/human_male_preview.json` (three representative
outfits), `configs/human_male.json` (all 43 parts), and the matching `_2h`, `_bow`
and `_staff` animation-family recipes. `human_male_catalog.json` records the
independently selectable source colors and meshes.

The standard delivery/import contract is described in [delivery.md](delivery.md).
